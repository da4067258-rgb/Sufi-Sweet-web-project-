About Sufi Sweets

Three generations of traditional sweet-making.

Our Story

Sufi Sweets began in 1994 as a small shop on Food Street, Lahore, founded by Haji Muhammad Sufi. His simple philosophy — use only pure desi ghee, fresh milk and honest ingredients — turned a single shop into one of the most loved sweet brands in the country.

Today the same recipes are followed in all twelve of our outlets, and the same care goes into every box we deliver to your door.

Our Journey

  • 1994 First shop opens on Food Street, Lahore with just three products.
  • 2003 Second outlet opens in Karachi; dairy line launched.
  • 2012 Gift hamper range introduced for weddings and festivals.
  • 2019 Reached 12 outlets across five cities.
  • 2026 Online store launched — sweets delivered to your doorstep.
